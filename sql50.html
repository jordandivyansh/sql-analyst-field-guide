Module 10 · SQL 50 System

Practice patterns, not memorized answers.

Use LeetCode SQL 50 as the question bank and this guide as the explanation layer. Every wrong answer becomes a reusable diagnostic rule.

4 weeks50 problemsSpaced reviewMySQL

One-problem protocol

  1. Restate: identify output grain, population, metric and ordering.
  2. Sketch: write the tables, join keys and intermediate grain.
  3. Attempt: work unaided for 20–25 minutes.
  4. Test: add mental cases for NULL, ties, duplicates, zeros and empty groups.
  5. Compare: study alternatives only after submitting your attempt.
  6. Compress: record a one-line pattern and your error category.
  7. Repeat: solve again after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days until clean.

Pattern index

Exclusion

NOT EXISTS, anti-join, NULL-safe logic.

At least N

GROUP BY + HAVING COUNT(*).

Latest / first

ROW_NUMBER per entity with a tie-breaker.

Top N per group

Aggregate, rank within partition, then filter.

Consecutive values

LAG or gaps-and-islands.

Share of total

Conditional sum or window aggregate with safe division.

Retention

Cohort date, period index, distinct active users.

Missing entities

Start from the full population and LEFT JOIN.

Deduplicate

Define business key, rank records, keep rn = 1.
